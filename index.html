PERSONAL SMART HOME DASHBOARD

CURIEL
COMMAND CENTER

A personal Android TV dashboard for an NVIDIA Shield: favorite applications, local time, weather, and authorized Google Nest live camera previews in one home screen.

Google Nest camera access

The app uses Google's official Device Access / Smart Device Management API. Its owner authorizes selected cameras through Google's consent screens. Device names identify the selected cards, and live WebRTC video is displayed on the owner's Shield. Google credentials are never entered into this website.

This public website provides application and privacy information only. It does not display camera feeds, host sign-in, or collect camera data.

Built for personal use

This is an independent household project, not a commercial service, public camera viewer, or Google product. Access to any camera requires its owner's explicit Google authorization. NVIDIA, Google, and Nest are trademarks of their respective owners.
